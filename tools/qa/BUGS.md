## B1 [S1] 4 of 5 demons are invisible in game and on the select screen (no fallback when the .glb is missing)
- **Repro:** `godot --path . res://scenes/main.tscn -- --demon=lilaeth --autopilot --shot=x.png --shot-time=30` (same for morvane, skarn, ysolde).
- **Evidence:** pass 1 `shots/lilaeth.png`, `morvane.png`, `skarn.png`, `ysolde.png`, `boss.png` (lilaeth): nothing is drawn at the player position (only weapon FX / damage numbers). `shots/select.png`: the four plinths other than Vharok's are empty. Log: `ERROR: Resource file not found: res://assets/models/demons/<id>.glb` + `WARNING: GlbModel: cannot load ...` (from `scripts/glb_model.gd:28/30`).
- **Cause:** only `assets/models/demons/vharok.glb` exists. `scenes/demons/demon_model.gd:75-78` builds `GlbModel.create(demon.get_model_path())` and simply `return`s when `scene_root == null` — no placeholder, so the player has no mesh at all.
- **Fix:** build the missing Blender models (`tools/blender/models/demons/<id>.py`), and/or have `DemonModel.build()` fall back to `vharok.glb` (tinted) or a primitive capsule when the file is missing so a missing asset is never an invisible player.

## B2 [S2] Autopilot never lets melee demons (Vharok, Ysolde) hit anything -> QA runs are unrepresentative
- **Repro:** `--autopilot --demon=vharok` (or ysolde), headless 45 s.
- **Evidence:** probe: vharok 6 kills / 45 s with 54 live enemies; ysolde 6 kills; boss run (vharok) 2 kills in 75 s with 71 enemies; versus 34-44 kills for lilaeth/morvane/skarn. `shots/vharok.png` at 00:29: "Angels slain: 0".
- **Cause:** `scenes/player/player.gd:226-240` (`_autopilot_input`) pushes away from any enemy within **4.0 m**, but Ravager's Cleave reach is `base_reach := 2.6` (`scenes/weapons/ravagers_cleave/ravagers_cleave.gd:18`), so the bot kites out of its own weapon's range. It's only a dev-tool problem, but it hides real balance/combat bugs for melee kits.
- **Fix:** scale the flee radius to the shortest weapon reach (e.g. `minf(4.0, reach * 0.8)`), or only flee when hp < 50 %.

## B3 [S2] Long run: Skarn (autopilot) dies at ~84 s; game-over then freezes the run
- **Repro:** `--autopilot --demon=skarn`, headless with `--fixed-fps 60`, 6 min.
- **Evidence:** pass 1 `logs/long.log`: hp 100 -> 38 at 58 s, `QA_PROBE_DIED t=84.0 kills=127 lvl=6`, then `paused=true` for the rest of the run. Difficulty ramp between 30 s and 80 s is steep for a 100-hp demon (it's at level 6 with only 2 weapons).
- **Note:** partly autopilot (it doesn't dodge well), but worth a balance look at spawn rules in `scenes/main.tscn` (EnemySpawner) for the 0:45-1:30 window. The suite now runs the long run with `--qa-immortal` (probe refills hp) so it covers the full 6 min.
